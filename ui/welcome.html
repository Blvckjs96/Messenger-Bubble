Good to know

  • Native notifications that respect Focus and Do Not Disturb.
  • Sharing your screen? Turn on Hide Bubble in Full Screen & Screen Sharing in the Bubble menu.
  • Quit with ⌘Q to keep your session signed in.
  • Saves battery by slowing down in the background.
